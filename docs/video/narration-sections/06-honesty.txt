To be straight about what I built: the CAD application is Chili3d, an excellent open-source project I did not write. What I built is the agent surface for it — seven tools, one gated write, and the safety layer around them. And it isn't a fork; not one upstream file is modified. Chili3d already loads plugins from its own origin, so Chisel is a genuine drop-in. That's the thing I'd want for any app: your CAD tool, your agent, no rewrite.
